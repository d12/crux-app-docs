# Browsing Climbs

## Viewing Climbs at Your Gym

To browse climbs in Crux, tap the big **"Climbs"** button on the main gym page. This opens the **climb list**, where you can scroll through all active climbs at your gym.

:::info
At gyms that post their own **official climbs**, the gym page has an **"Official climbs"** button for the gym's climbs and a **"Custom climbs"** button for climbs set by climbers. See [Gym-Set Climbs](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/about-crux/optional-feature-gym-set-climbs.md).
:::

By default, climbs are **sorted by set date**, with the most recently set climbs appearing first.

---

## Searching by Name or Setter

To **search for a climb**, type a **climb name** or **setter name** in the **"Search by name or setter..."** box at the top of the list. The list filters as you type: a climb **matches** if either its name or its setter's name contains your search term.

---

## Using the Sort & Filter Menu

To **refine your search**, use the **"Browse climbs"** menu:
- Tap the **filter icon** next to the search bar.
- Choose your **sort and filter options**.
- Tap **"Search"** to apply them (or **"Reset"** to start over).

A small red dot on the filter icon reminds you when filters are on.

### Sorting Options

You can **sort climbs** by:
- **Area** – Groups climbs by wall or area (only at gyms with more than one).
- **Angle** – Only for [adjustable walls](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/homewall-specific-features/adustable-angle-walls.md).
- **Set date** – Shows the most recent climbs first.
- **Grade** – Lists climbs from **easiest to hardest**.
- **Rating** – Displays the **highest-rated** climbs first.
- **Sends** – Shows the most **frequently sent** climbs first.

Turn on **"Reverse sort"** to flip the order.

### Filtering Options

You can **filter climbs** by:
- **Sent status** – **All climbs**, climbs you've **Sent**, or climbs you've **Not sent yet**.
- **Active status** – **Active** climbs (on the wall now), **Archived** climbs (taken down), or **All climbs**.
- **Climbs with beta videos** – Show only climbs with **beta videos**.
- **Area** – Filter by wall or area.
- **Angle** – For adjustable walls.
- **Grade** – Pick one or more grades.
- **Tags** – Filter climbs by **tags** (e.g., "Slopey", "Dynamic").
- **Hold color** – On a gym's official climbs.

---

## Saving Your Filter Settings

If you often use the same filters, you can **save them as your default**:
1. Open the **Browse climbs** menu.
2. Adjust your preferred settings.
3. Tap **"Set as default"** (top right of the menu).

Your settings will **automatically apply** the next time you open the climb list. To go back to the normal view, open the menu and tap **"Clear saved filter"**.

---

## Swiping Between Climbs

On a climb page, you can **swipe left or right** to move to the next or previous climb in the list you opened it from, in that list's sort and filter order.

📌 **Note:** This works only on the **climb page**, not in the climb list.
