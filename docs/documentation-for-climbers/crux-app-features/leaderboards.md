# Leaderboards

Crux features **friendly gym leaderboards** to help climbers track progress and compete with friends. Tap **"Leaderboards"** on the gym page to see them. There are two tabs:

- **Climbers** – Ranks climbers by **most sends**.
- **Setters** – Ranks setters by the **likes** or the **sends** their climbs have received from other climbers (a setter's own likes and sends don't count, and repeats count once).

Choose the time window at the top: **1 week**, **30 days** (the default) or **1 year**. Each board shows the top 10.

<img src="/img/leaderboard.png" alt="Climber leaderboards on the Crux Climbing App." class="screenshot" />

:::tip
Prefer to stay off the boards? Turn on **"Hide my name from leaderboards"** in your profile's **Privacy settings**.
:::

:::info
Leaderboards aren't available at gyms on the free tier.
:::
