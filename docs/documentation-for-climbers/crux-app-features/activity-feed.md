# Gym Activity Feed

The **gym activity feed** is the **"Recent activity"** section of the gym page, below the gym's buttons (on a tablet or computer, it's the column on the right). It shows the most recent activity at your gym, including **announcements, new climbs, beta videos, and more**.

---

## What Appears in the Activity Feed?

The following types of activity are displayed in the feed:

- **Gym Announcements** – Gym staff can post updates about **events, gym hour changes, and other news**.
- **Crux Announcements** – Occasionally, the **Crux team** posts announcements about **new features** or **important updates**.
- **New Climbs** – Climbs that have just been posted at the gym.
- **Sends** – Climbs your fellow climbers have sent.
- **Photos, Beta Videos & Comments** – When a climber **posts a photo or beta video** or **comments on a climb**, it appears in the feed.
- **Milestones** – Climb milestones (e.g., **10 sends or 10 likes**) are highlighted in the feed.
- **Climber Posts** – Climbers can post in the feed to **ask questions or start discussions**.

To post to the feed, type in the **"What's on your mind?"** box at the top of the feed (you can add a photo) and tap **"Post"**.

:::tip
Don't want your activity in the feed? Turn on **"Hide my content in the activity feed"** in your profile's **Privacy settings**.
:::

---

## Pinned Items

Gym admins can **pin** up to **two** feed items to the top of the activity feed to make sure important updates are seen.

To pin an item:
1. Tap the **⋮** in the top-right corner of the post.
2. Select **"Pin to top."**

:::info
Pinned items stay at the top of the feed until they're unpinned (**⋮ → "Unpin"**).
:::
