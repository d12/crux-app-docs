# Recording Your Sends

Tracking your sends in Crux helps you **monitor your progress** over time and browse past climbing sessions on your profile page.

---

## Counting Attempts

At the bottom of every climb page, next to the send button, there's an **attempts counter** (**− N attempts +**). Tap **+** each time you try the climb. Attempts are saved to today's session even if you don't send, so you can see what you've been projecting.

Once you've sent a climb, the climb page shows how many times you've sent it and how many attempts your first send took. Tap that line to **update the attempts count**.

---

## How to Mark Sends

1. Open the **climb page** for the climb you sent.
2. Tap **"Mark as sent"** at the bottom of the page.
3. Fill out the send details:
   - **How many attempts did it take?**
   - **When did you send this climb?** (today by default)
   - **Suggested grade** (optional)
   - **Climb rating** (optional, 1–5 stars)
4. Tap **"Mark as sent"** to log your send.

<img src="/img/mark-as-sent-dialog.png" alt="Marking a climb as sent on the Crux Climbing App." class="screenshot" />

After logging a send, you can tap **"Share"** to share it as an image.

On an [adjustable wall](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/homewall-specific-features/adustable-angle-walls.md), pick the angle you climbed at from the angle chips on the climb page first: the send, your attempts and any suggested grade are recorded at that angle (**"Mark as sent at 45°"**). Each angle is its own tick, so your first send at a new angle is a new send, not a repeat.

---

## Suggested Grades

You can **suggest a grade** when logging a send (at gyms that re-grade climbs by consensus).

:::info
Climb grades **adjust over time** based on climber consensus: the grade is the average of the setter's proposed grade and climbers' suggestions.
:::

---

## Marking Repeats

After logging your **first send** of a climb, the **"Mark as sent"** button changes to **"Log repeat"**.

Use this to **log additional sends** of the same climb.

---

## Undoing a Send

Logged a send by mistake? Tap the **⋮** at the top right of the climb page and choose **"Undo recent send"**.
