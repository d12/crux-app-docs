# Sharing Photos and Beta Videos

Crux allows climbers to **share photos and beta videos** with the community. Whether you're celebrating a send, looking for beta, or helping others figure out a climb, sharing visuals makes the experience more interactive!

---

## How to Share Photos and Beta Videos

To share a photo or beta video:
1. Open the **climb page** for the climb you want to post about.
2. Stay on the **"Activity"** tab below the climb.
3. Tap the **round button** in the bottom right.
4. In **"Add a comment"**, write in **"Share your thoughts on this climb"** and tap the **camera** or **video** button to attach a photo or video. If someone else is climbing in the video, say who under **"Who's climbing?"**.
5. Tap **"Post"**.

<img src="/img/posting-comments.png" alt="Posting a comment on a climb on the Crux Climbing App." class="screenshot" />

:::info
Beta videos aren't available at gyms on the free tier.
:::

---

## Discovering Photos and Beta Videos

📍 **On Climb Pages**
Photos and beta videos are shown in the **Activity** tab of each climb. Filter the climb list by **"Climbs with beta videos"** to find climbs that have them.

📍 **In the Gym Activity Feed**
Photos and videos also appear in the gym's [**Activity Feed**](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/crux-app-features/activity-feed.md), making it easy to browse **recently shared content** from your gym.

📍 **New Beta Notifications**
Tap the **bell** next to a climb's photo to get a push notification when someone posts new beta for it.

---

## Replies, Fist Bumps and Reports

- Tap **"Add a reply"** under a comment to reply to it.
- Give a comment a **fist bump** to show some love.
- See something inappropriate? Tap the **⋮** on the comment and choose **"Report"** (the Crux team reviews it) or **"Block"** the person.

---

## Managing Your Comments

You can **delete** your comments at any time:
1. Tap the **⋮** in the top-right corner of your comment.
2. Select **"Delete"** to remove it.

A climb's setter and the gym's staff can also **pin** a helpful comment to the top.
