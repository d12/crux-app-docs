# Downloading the Crux App and Signing In

## Downloading Crux

Crux is free to download on both iOS and Android:

- [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/climbwithcrux/id6448704757)
- [Download on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ca.cruxapp.twa)

Need to share the download link? Use [https://www.cruxapp.ca/download](https://www.cruxapp.ca/download) to direct users to the right store for their device.

Crux also works in any web browser at [https://www.cruxapp.ca/app](https://www.cruxapp.ca/app).

## Signing In

Sign in with your **Apple** or **Google** account by tapping **"Sign in with Apple"** or **"Sign in with Google"**. Your Crux data is linked to this account, so be sure to always use the same sign-in method.

<img src="/img/login.png" alt="Signing in to the Crux app" class="screenshot" />

### Browsing Without an Account

You can look around before signing in: gyms, climbs, climb lists, profiles and leaderboards are all open to browse. When you try something that needs an account, like logging a send or posting a comment, Crux asks you to sign in.

### Staying Signed In
- After signing in once, you won't need to sign in again unless you log out or delete the app.
- To log out, go to your **profile**, tap the **settings cog** in the top right, and scroll down to **"Log out"** under **Account**.

:::tip
Opened Crux from a link inside another app (like Instagram)? Google sign-in doesn't work in those in-app browsers. Open the link in your phone's browser instead, or use **Sign in with Apple**.
:::
