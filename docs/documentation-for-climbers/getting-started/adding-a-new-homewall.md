# Adding a New Homewall

## How Much Does It Cost?

Adding a homewall to Crux is completely free! Homewalls get full access to all features, including unlimited walls, resets, climbs and setters.

## Who Can Set on My Homewall?

By default, only you can set climbs on your homewall. You can add other setters, or let anyone set. See the [Adding and Removing Setters guide](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/homewall-specific-features/adding-and-removing-setters.md).

## How to Add Your Homewall

1. **Start the process** – Tap **"Add a commercial gym or home wall"** on the gym selection page. (See [Selecting Your Gym](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/getting-started/selecting-your-gym.md) for details.)
2. **Tell us who you are** – Choose **"I'm a climber"** and tap **"Next"**.
3. **Enter homewall details** – Provide your homewall's name and (optionally) its location. Select **"Home climbing wall"** as the gym type.
4. **(Optional) Add a banner image** – This is decorative only and won't affect route setting.
5. **Choose a grade scale** – Select **V grades**, **Font grades**, or **Custom**.
6. **Review and confirm** – Double-check the details on **"Review your gym"**, then tap **"Add gym"**. That's it!

## Adding Your Wall Photo

Once your homewall is created, tap the **"Gym walls"** button on its page (marked **"Start here!"**) and add a photo of your wall. Crux finds every hold on it, so setting a climb is just tapping holds.
