# Adding a New Commercial Gym

## Who Can Add a Commercial Gym?

Anyone can add a commercial gym to Crux—you don't need to be a gym staff member. When you add a gym, you'll initially be listed as its admin in Crux. If the gym's staff later want to run it on Crux, they can [claim it and start a free trial](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/getting-your-gym-started-with-crux.md).

:::info
Work at the gym? Choose **"I work at a commercial climbing gym"** in the first step instead. You'll start a free 14-day trial of Crux Pro for your gym. See [Getting Your Gym Started with Crux](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/getting-your-gym-started-with-crux.md).
:::

## How Much Does It Cost?

Adding a commercial gym to Crux is completely free. Gyms added by climbers are on the **free tier**: each climber can set up to **2 climbs per day**, on the gym's walls. If the gym wants to unlock the full app, it can choose a [paid plan](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/about-crux/free-tier-vs-paid-plans.md).

## How to Add a Commercial Gym

1. **Start the process** – Tap **"Add a commercial gym or home wall"** on the gym selection page. (For more details, see [Selecting Your Gym](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/getting-started/selecting-your-gym.md).)
2. **Tell us who you are** – Choose **"I'm a climber"** and tap **"Next"**.
3. **Enter gym details** – Provide the gym's name and (optionally) its location. Select **"Commercial climbing gym"** as the gym type.
4. **(Optional) Add a banner image** – This is purely decorative and won't affect route setting.
5. **Choose a grade scale** – Select **V grades**, **Font grades**, or **Custom**.
6. **Review and confirm** – Double-check the details on **"Review your gym"**, then tap **"Add gym"**. Done!
