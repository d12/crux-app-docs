# Adjustable Angle Walls for Homewalls

:::warning
**This feature is only available for our supporters on Patreon** (any tier).
[Learn more about supporting Crux on Patreon.](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/support-crux-on-patreon.md)
:::

The **adjustable angle** feature allows you to **assign a board angle** to each climb on your homewall. This makes it easier to **search, sort, and browse climbs** based on their angle.

---

## Enabling the Feature

You can turn it on yourself for each wall:
1. On your homewall page, tap **"Gym walls"**.
2. Tap the wall, then **"Rename or change settings"** (or add a new wall).
3. Tick **"Wall angle is adjustable"**.
4. Choose the wall's **"Minimum angle"** and **"Maximum angle"** (from 0°, vertical, up to 70°, in 5° steps).

---

## Assigning an Angle to a Climb

When setting a climb on an adjustable wall, choose its **angle** in the **"Angle"** field.

<img src="/img/angle-when-setting-climb.png" alt="When setting a climb on a homewall with the adjustable angle feature enabled, there is a field to select an angle." class="screenshot" />

---

## Browsing Climbs by Angle

In the **climb list**, each climb displays its assigned **angle** beside its name.

<img src="/img/angle-in-climb-list.png" alt="When browsing climbs in the climb list, climbs have an angle next to the name." class="screenshot" />

### Filtering by Angle
You can **sort and filter climbs by angle**:
1. Tap the **filter icon** next to the search bar.
2. Choose an **angle range**, or pick **Angle** under **"Sort by"**.

<img src="/img/sort-filter-by-angles.png" alt="With adjustable angles enabled on your homewall, you can filter and sort by angle using the filter menu." class="screenshot" />
