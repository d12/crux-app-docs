# Importing from Another App

Moving to Crux from **Stōkt** or **Retroflash**? Crux can copy a wall and its climbs into your gym, so your climbers have something to climb on day one.

To start, go to **Gym Settings → "Import from another app"** (under **Walls**) and pick the app you're moving from:
- **Stōkt** – Spray and board walls
- **Retroflash** – System and home walls

You can also choose **"Are you moving from another app?"** when you [start your trial](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/getting-your-gym-started-with-crux.md#starting-your-free-trial).

---

## How It Works

1. **Find the gym** on the other app by name (or paste a Retroflash profile link).
2. **Pick the wall** to copy.
3. **Check what will be copied**, tick **"This is my wall, or the gym agreed to me copying it."**, and tap **"Start import"**.

Crux copies:
- A new wall with its **photo and every hold outline** (and every photo of the wall, oldest to newest).
- Every climb, with its **name, grade, holds, feet rule, description and date**. Climbs with a grade your gym doesn't use come over as **Ungraded**, so you can grade them later.

These stay in the other app: climbers' names, sends, comments and likes, and who set each climb (copied climbs are posted under the name of whoever starts the import).

An import takes a few minutes. You can leave the page: the import keeps going, and you'll find it under **"Imports into this gym"** on the same page. To copy more walls, tap **"Import another wall"**.
