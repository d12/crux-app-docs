# Gym Analytics

Your gym's **Analytics** page shows who uses Crux at your gym and how your climbs are doing. It's available to your gym's **admins**.

To open it, go to **Gym Settings → "Analytics"** (under **Insights and promotion**), or tap the **"Analytics"** button on your gym page.

Choose a period at the top: **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.

## What's on the Page

- **Summary** – How many climbers used Crux at your gym, with **New climbers**, climbers who **Came back**, **Sends logged** and **Sessions**, each compared with the previous period.
- **Week by week** – Climbers and sends over the last 12 weeks. Tap a week for its numbers.
- **Busy times** – Your busiest days and hours, from the last 90 days.
- **What's set and what's climbed** – Each grade's share of the climbs up now, next to its share of sends. Crux points out grades climbers might like more of.
- **Climbs to look at** – The climbs up now that are **Most sent**, **Top rated** and **Most talked about**, climbs with **Grade feedback** (where 2 or more climbers suggested a different grade), and **Quiet climbs** that might be ready to come down.
- **Climber feedback** – Comments, ratings, grade votes, followers and **poster scans**.
- **Since you joined Crux** – Your gym's climbers, sends and climbs posted, all time.
