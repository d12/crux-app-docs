# Moderation and Administration Tools

Gym staff have access to **moderation tools** to help manage their gym's content on Crux. These tools allow staff to:
- Manage **climbs and climb lists**
- Pin and remove **posts in the activity feed**
- Pin helpful **comments**

---

## Who Can Moderate Content?

Only **gym staff** listed in the
[**Staff members section**](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/gym-settings-and-staff-members.md#staff-members)
can moderate content on your gym's page. On Pro, what each staff member can do depends on their role.

---

## How Moderation Works

Most moderation actions are in the **⋮ menu** on the content you want to manage.

---

## Moderation Abilities

### Climbs
Gym staff can, under **"Climb admin"** in a climb's ⋮ menu:
- **Edit climbs** (image, name, description, grade and more) with **"Edit climb"**.
- **Archive** or **restore** climbs ("Archive climb" / "Restore climb").
- **Delete climbs** ("Delete climb"). Deleting removes every send of the climb, so prefer archiving unless a climb is dangerous or offensive.
- **Make a climb a draft** or **publish** it.
- **Add or remove climbs** from the gym's
  [**public climb lists**](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/crux-app-features/climb-lists.md), and **edit or delete** them.

Climbers can **report an issue** with a climb (no longer on the wall, wrong grade, blurry photo, duplicate…), and staff can act on it.

### Activity Feed
Gym staff can:
- **Delete posts** in the gym's activity feed.
- **Pin** up to two posts to the top of the feed (admins) (**"Pin to top"** / **"Unpin"**).

### Comments and Videos
- Staff and a climb's setter can **pin** a comment to the top of a climb's activity (**"Pin to top"**).
- Anyone can **report** a comment or video (the Crux team reviews it) or **block** its author.

Need a comment removed, or have a problem with a climber? [Contact the Crux team](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/about-crux/contact-the-crux-team.md).
