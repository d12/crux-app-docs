# Gym Settings and Staff Members

:::info
Gym settings are only available to your gym's **admins** (see [Staff Members](#staff-members) below).
:::

To open your **gym settings**:
1. Open your **gym page**.
2. Tap the **"Settings"** button (or the **settings cog** at the top left of the gym's banner).

<img src="/img/gym-settings.png" alt="The gym settings page on the Crux Climbing App" class="screenshot" />

The **Gym Settings** page has these sections:

| Section | Settings |
| --- | --- |
| **Gym** | **Gym info**, **Personalization** (banner and logo), **Staff members** |
| **Climbs** | **Grades**, **Climb appearance**, **Official climbs** (Pro), **Climb tags**, **Feet and matching rules** |
| **Walls** | **Climbing walls**, **Reset or clear a wall**, **Import from another app** |
| **Insights and promotion** | **Analytics**, **Posters and social media** |
| **Help and support** | **Help & Support** (email the Crux team) |
| **Developer settings** | **Gym webhooks** |
| **Billing and account** | **Manage payment**, **Delete gym** (only for new gyms) |

---

## Gym Info

The **Gym info** section lets you change how your gym appears in the app. You can edit:
- **Gym name** (and a **short name**)
- **Location**
- **Description**
- **Public email** (for climbers to contact you)

It also has two switches:
- **"Automatically re-grade climbs based on climber consensus"**
- **"Climbers can post climbs on photos"** – Turn this off to limit climbers' custom climbs to your spray walls (Standard and Pro).

Your gym's **banner** and **logo** are under **Personalization**.

---

## Grades, Tags and Rules

- **Grades** – Rename, reorder, or recolor your grades.
- **Climb appearance** – Choose how climbs are drawn for everyone at your gym (outline style, thickness, labels, hold colors).
- **Climb tags** – Add your own tags for climbers to use.
- **Feet and matching rules** – Turn feet rules on or off, or add your own.

## Staff Members

The **Staff members** section lets you **add or remove staff members** from your gym:
1. Tap **"Add a staff member"** and search for their name. (They need to have joined Crux and set a name in their profile.)
2. To remove someone, tap the **trash icon** next to their name.

What staff can do depends on your plan:

**Pro:** give each staff member a **role**:
- **Admin** – Everything, including settings, billing and staff.
- **Staff** – Walls, spray-wall climbs, unsetting and moderation. No settings, billing or staff.
- **Setter** – Creates and manages [official climbs](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/about-crux/optional-feature-gym-set-climbs.md).

**Standard:** every staff member is an **admin**.

**Older plans and the free tier:** staff can manage climbs and moderate content; tick **"Admin"** to also give them the gym settings.
