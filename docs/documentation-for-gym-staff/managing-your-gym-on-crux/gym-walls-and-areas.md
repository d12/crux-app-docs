# Gym Walls and Areas

Crux offers two main ways to organize your climbs: **spray walls** and **areas**. This page explains the difference between the two, and how to get started.

## Spray walls

When you create a spray wall, you add one photo of the wall and Crux finds every hold on it. When climbers set a climb on a spray wall, they just tap holds on that photo. There's no need for climbers to take a photo of the wall they're setting on.

This is similar to the experience offered by other spray wall apps, except you can update your spray wall photo any time for free.

### When to use spray walls

Like the name suggests, spray walls are great for your spray walls and boards. If you can fit the wall in one photo and it doesn't change too often, it should probably be a spray wall.

### Important notes

- You can have as many spray walls as you like on **Standard** and **Pro**. On the older **spray wall plan**, the number of spray walls is part of your plan: tap **"Add more spray walls"** on the walls page to add more.
- Gyms on the **free tier** can have up to **2 walls**. Climbers who climb there regularly can add them and put up new photos when holds change.

## Areas

Areas don't have a photo attached to them. Instead, they organize climbs in your gym. Climbers set climbs anywhere in the gym by taking a photo, and assign them to an area.

Areas are great for organizing your gym into sections like the bouldering cave or the comp wall. Climbers can easily filter climbs by area to find what they're looking for.

### When to use areas

Use areas for sections of your gym that don't fit into one photo or that change frequently. Generally, the regular-set walls at a gym should be areas in Crux.

### Important notes

- Areas are available on **Standard**, **Pro** and the older **whole gym** plan.
- You can have as many areas as you want, but make sure climbers know the name of each area, since they choose one when setting a climb.

## Getting started with walls

To add walls to your gym, open your gym's **Settings** and tap **"Climbing walls"**. The **Gym Walls** page has **Spray walls** and **Areas** tabs, with **"Add a spray wall"** and **"Add an area"** buttons.

## Updating a wall

Added holds, or reset a wall? Tap the wall on the Gym Walls page, then choose what changed:
- **Rename or change settings** – The wall's name (and angle, for adjustable walls).
- **Fix the hold outlines** – Remove false detections or outline missed holds.
- **Add or move holds** – Take a new photo. Climbs stay up.
- **Reset the wall** – Take a new photo. Current climbs are archived.
- **Archive all the climbs** – The photo stays.
- **Remove the wall** – Its climbs are archived.

Updating a wall is free, and climbs, sends and stats are always kept: older climbs keep using the photo they were set on. See [Unsetting Climbs](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/unsetting-climbs.md).
