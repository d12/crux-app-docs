# Posters and Social Media

Crux makes **posters** and **social media images** for your gym. Each one shows your gym's name and one of your climbs, so climbers recognise your wall, and posters have a **QR code** that takes climbers straight to your gym in Crux.

To find them, go to **Gym Settings → "Posters and social media"** (under **Insights and promotion**). Only your gym's admins can download them.

## Posters

Pick a poster, a **poster language** and a size (**Letter**, **A4**, **11×17** or **A3**), then tap **"Download PDF"**. Crux recommends the posters that suit your gym:
- **Spray wall poster** (and a plainer **Spray wall poster, clean**) – For a spray wall or board.
- **Custom climbs poster** – Shows climbers browsing and setting their own climbs.
- **Whole gym poster** – Shows climbers your climbs, sends and what's new.

:::tip Printing tips
- Print at **100% (actual size)**, not "fit to page". Letter and A4 work at the front desk; 11×17 and A3 on the walls.
- Print in colour on **matte** paper: glossy paper reflects light and makes the QR code harder to scan.
- Hang posters at eye level where climbers rest, near the spray wall and by the front desk. Test the QR code with your phone once it's up.
:::

Your [analytics](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/gym-analytics.md) count how many times your posters' QR codes are scanned.

## Social Media Images

The **"Social media images"** tab has ready-made images for your gym's Instagram, Facebook or X account, in the language you pick. Each comes in four sizes:
- **Story** (1080 × 1920) – Instagram and Facebook stories
- **Portrait** (1080 × 1350) – Instagram and Facebook posts
- **Landscape** (1200 × 630) – Facebook, X and LinkedIn posts
- **Square** (1080 × 1080) – Any feed

Tap **"Download"** to save an image, and **"Copy caption"** to copy a suggested caption to go with it.
