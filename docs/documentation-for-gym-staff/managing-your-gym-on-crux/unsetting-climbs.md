# Unsetting Climbs

In Crux, taking climbs down is called **archiving**. Archived climbs disappear from the climb list, but they **keep their sends, comments and ratings**, so climbers' stats don't change. You can restore any archived climb from its page.

---

## Resetting or Clearing a Wall

To take down the climbs on one wall:
1. Open your gym's **Settings** and tap **"Reset or clear a wall"**. This opens the **Gym Walls** page. (Walls are explained in [Gym Walls and Areas](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/gym-walls-and-areas.md).)
2. Tap the wall.
3. Choose what happened:
   - **"Reset the wall"** – Take a photo of the reset wall. The climbs that are up now are archived, and new climbs are set on the new photo.
   - **"Add or move holds"** – Take a new photo after a partial reset. Crux compares it with the old photo and shows which climbs use holds that were taken off; the climbs you tick are archived and the rest **stay up**.
   - **"Archive all the climbs"** – Keep the photo, but archive every climb on it.

## Archiving Every Climb in the Gym

Gym admins can archive every current climb at once with **"Archive every climb in the gym"** at the bottom of the Gym Walls page.

---

## Archiving Some Climbs

- **One climb:** open it and choose **⋮ → "Archive climb"**.
- **Several climbs:** select them in the climb list, then archive them together.

To see archived climbs, set **Active status** to **"Archived"** in the climb list's filter menu. To bring one back, open it and choose **⋮ → "Restore climb"**.
