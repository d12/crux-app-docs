# Announcements and Push Notifications

Crux lets you send **free-form push notifications** directly to your climbers.

Since **emails often end up in spam** and **social media can be unreliable**, push notifications provide a **direct and effective way** to communicate with your community.

---

## Who Receives Push Notifications?

When you post an announcement as your gym, climbers who:
- **Climbed at your gym in the last two weeks**, and
- Have **push notifications on** (including **"Gym announcements"** in their notification settings)

…receive a push notification: **"New announcement from (Your Gym Name)"**. The announcement also appears in your gym's activity feed for everyone.

Want to know how many climbers are receiving or opening your notifications?
[Email us](mailto:contact@cruxapp.ca), and we'll provide insights!

---

## Who Can Send Announcements?

Any **gym staff member** listed in the [Staff Members section](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/gym-settings-and-staff-members.md) can send announcements. Announcements aren't available to gyms on the free tier.

---

## How to Send an Announcement

Announcements are posted in the **"Recent activity"** feed on your gym's main page.

### Steps to Post an Announcement:
1. Open the **Crux app** and navigate to your **gym's main page**.
2. Write your **announcement** in the **"What's on your mind?"** box. _(Optional: Add a photo.)_
3. Tap the **arrow next to the "Post" button** and select **"Post as (Your Gym Name)."** _(Posting as yourself doesn't send notifications.)_
4. Tap **"Post"**, then **"Yes, post and notify climbers"**.

That's it!
