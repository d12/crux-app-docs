# Billing and Pricing

---

## Pricing

Crux has two plans for commercial gyms, both priced **per gym** with **unlimited walls**:

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standard** | $25 per month | $240 per year ($20 per month) |
| **Pro** | $60 per month | $600 per year ($50 per month) |

- Prices are the same figure in **USD, CAD, EUR and GBP**; checkout shows your currency.
- Paying yearly **saves up to 20%**.
- See [Free Tier vs. Paid Plans](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/about-crux/free-tier-vs-paid-plans.md) for what each plan includes.
- Running several gyms? [Contact us](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/about-crux/contact-the-crux-team.md) about a contract for your chain.

Home walls are free, and Crux is **always free for climbers**.

---

## Free Trial

Every commercial gym gets a **free 14-day trial of Crux Pro**, with every feature and **no credit card needed**. See [Getting Your Gym Started with Crux](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/getting-your-gym-started-with-crux.md).

You can choose a plan at any time during the trial. If you pay before the trial ends, you **keep the rest of your trial**: your first charge is on the day the trial would have ended.

---

## Choosing a Plan

Gym admins choose a plan on the gym's **plans page** in the Crux app:
1. Open your gym page. During a trial, tap **"See plans"** (or **"Upgrade now"**) in the trial banner.
2. Pick **Standard** or **Pro**, and **Annual** or **Monthly** billing.
3. Tap **"Continue to Payment"** and pay securely with Stripe.

---

## Billing Structure

- Crux is a **month-to-month or yearly subscription** with **no long-term commitment**.
- The **gym is billed directly**—there are **no fees for your climbers**.
- You can **cancel at any time**. If you cancel partway through a billing period, email [contact@cruxapp.ca](mailto:contact@cruxapp.ca) for a prorated refund of the unused time.

To update your card, see invoices, or change your plan, open **Gym Settings → "Manage payment"**.
