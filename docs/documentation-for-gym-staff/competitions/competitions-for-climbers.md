# Competitions for Climbers

Here's what your climbers see, so you can explain it at the start of your comp.

---

## Finding and Joining

Climbers find your competitions on your **gym's page** in Crux. A live competition shows near the top of the page. Each competition has its own page with:
- When it runs and how many people are competing.
- **How scoring works**, written out in plain words from your climbs' points.
- Your description and rules.

<img src="/img/competitions/competition-info.png" alt="A competition's page in Crux: live now, how scoring works, the description and the rules" class="screenshot" />

Climbers tap **Join** (and pick their division, if there are divisions). They can join any time until the competition ends. Without an account, people can still look at a competition and its leaderboard.

---

## Scoring on Their Phone

During the competition, the **Climbs** tab is the climber's scorecard. Each climb shows its photo and number, with buttons:
- **Attempt**: one more attempt.
- **Zone**: they reached the zone (only on climbs with a zone).
- **Top**: they topped it. When a top's points depend on the attempt (a flash bonus or points off per attempt), the button shows what a top is worth right now, like *Top · 100*.

An accidental top can be undone straight away, and tapping a climb's result lets them correct it. Their points, their place and how many climbs they've topped stay at the top of the screen.

<img src="/img/competitions/competition-scorecard.png" alt="A climber's competition scorecard in Crux: their points and place, with flashes, tops, and Attempt and Top buttons" class="screenshot" />

Every top also goes into the climber's **logbook**, so their stats keep counting. (A test competition never touches anyone's logbook.)

---

## The Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** tab ranks everyone (in each division) and updates as climbers score. Tap a row to see that climber's result on each climb.

<img src="/img/competitions/competition-leaderboard.png" alt="A live competition leaderboard in Crux, ranked by points, with tops and flashes" class="screenshot" />

When the competition ends, the leaderboard is final, and competitors get a notification saying where they placed.
