# Getting Your Gym Started with Crux

Welcome to Crux! We're excited to have you on board. This guide will help you **get started** and make the most of the platform.

---

## Setting Up Your Gym

### Starting Your Free Trial

Every commercial gym gets a **free 14-day trial of Crux Pro**, with every feature and **no credit card needed**. You can set everything up yourself, in your browser or in the app:

1. Go to [https://www.cruxapp.ca/app/start](https://www.cruxapp.ca/app/start) (or tap **"Start free trial"** on [cruxapp.ca](https://www.cruxapp.ca)).
2. **Sign in** with Google or Apple.
3. **"Is your gym already on Crux?"** Search for your gym:
   - If climbers already added it, tap **"Claim"** (or **"Set up"**). Your climbers and their climbs stay as they are.
   - If it isn't on Crux yet, tap **"Add your gym"** and fill in its details.
4. Your trial starts, and you're the gym's first **admin**.

:::tip
Moving from **Stōkt** or **Retroflash**? Answer **"Are you moving from another app?"** on the start page and Crux copies your walls and climbs for you. You can also do it later from your settings: see [Importing from Another App](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/importing-from-another-app.md).
:::

During the trial, your gym page shows a **setup checklist** with the days left in your trial:
- **Photograph a wall**
- **Set your first climbs**
- **Add your logo and banner**
- **Put up your QR posters**
- **Reach 10 active climbers**

### Choosing a Plan

When your trial ends, choose **Standard** or **Pro** from the trial banner on your gym page (**"See plans"**). If you choose neither, your gym moves to the free plan. See [Billing and Pricing](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/about-crux/billing-and-pricing.md).

### Installing the Crux App

Crux works in any browser, but the app makes it easy to photograph walls and set climbs at the gym. Follow the instructions [here](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/getting-started/downloading-crux-and-signing-in.md) to install it on your phone, and sign in with the same account.

### Adding Your Staff

:::info
Staff members **must download and sign in to Crux** (and set a name in their profile) before you can add them to your gym.
:::

Once your gym is set up, you can start **adding staff**:
1. Open your **gym page** and tap **"Settings"**.
2. Select **"Staff members"**.
3. Tap **"Add a staff member"** and search for their name.

On **Pro**, give each staff member a **role** (Admin, Staff or Setter). On **Standard**, every staff member is an admin. See [Gym Settings and Staff Members](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/gym-settings-and-staff-members.md#staff-members).

### Adding Your Walls

If you have a spray wall or board, add it before climbers begin using the app. You only need one straight-on photo: Crux finds the holds.

To add a wall:
1. Open your **gym page** and tap **"Settings"**.
2. Select **"Climbing walls"**.
3. Tap **"Add a spray wall"** (or **"Add an area"** for parts of the gym that change often) and follow the steps.

See [Gym Walls and Areas](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/gym-walls-and-areas.md) for more.

### Posting Initial Climbs

Before announcing Crux to your climbers, it's helpful to have some climbs set. This helps new users **understand Crux's purpose** when they first install the app.

To add climbs:
1. Open your **gym page** and tap **"Climbs"**.
2. Tap the round **"+"** button.
3. Follow the steps. See [Setting Climbs](/docs/documentation-for-climbers/crux-app-features/setting-climbs.md).

If you have a **spray wall**, we recommend setting at least:
- **One spray climb**
- **One regular wall climb**

## Announcing Crux to Your Climbers

### Choosing a Launch Date

The **exact date** isn't critical, but we recommend **selecting a single launch date** to:
- Put up **posters**
- Announce on **social media**

A single launch date **builds excitement** and increases engagement.

### Posters and Social Media

Crux makes **QR code posters** and **social media images** for your gym, with your gym's name and a climb from your wall. Find them in **Gym Settings → "Posters and social media"**. See [Posters and Social Media](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/posters-and-social-media.md).

We recommend **posting on all available channels**, such as:
- **Instagram**
- **Facebook**
- **Email newsletters**

#### Examples of previous Crux launch posts:
- [Altitude Gym Kanata](https://www.instagram.com/p/DE2_dxIMJ9l/)
- [Pursuit Climbing](https://www.instagram.com/p/DAG2-nou9J5/)

### Launch Day Checklist

On the **morning of your launch date**, make sure to:
- **Put up posters**
  - At least one **near the front desk** (so climbers can ask about Crux).
  - One **near the spray wall**, if applicable.
- **Post on social media** to announce the launch.
- **Post an announcement** in the app to welcome your first climbers. See [Announcements and Push Notifications](/docs/documentation-for-gym-staff/managing-your-gym-on-crux/announcements-and-push-notifications.md).
